## [Unreleased]

### Added
- 2026-07-29 — **The global dynamical core is now the weather** (atmosphere phase C3): `SynopticLayer` — eight authored pressure-system ellipses with headings, lifecycle timers and drawn front rays — is deleted, and T1 drives the regional nest, the editor, the gameplay wind and the scene file. Re-measured through the shipped provider with **nothing injected**, the core grows eddies exponentially at **0.254/day over days 8–20** (matching C2's independent 0.26/day), draws the zonal flow down from 704 000 to 466 000 J/m² and hands it back, peaks at 42 m/s and 0.235 mm/day of rain, then decays — a baroclinic life cycle, unscripted. A `FrontPassage` preset produces a **measured front passage** over the observer: the anomaly deepens overhead, passes, the ridge builds behind it (−90 → +29 hPa), and the wind **veers from north-northeast to nearly due west** while the thermal gradient sharpens from 1.02 to 1.29 K/100 km.
  - Retargeted the Davies forcing onto real fields. Three interim constructions went out with the analytic layer: a stylized warm/cold sector mask driving the anomalies, the four constants describing how strong that sector was, and C1's Ekman-pumping estimate of the vertical motion — the core supplies all three directly, the last as its quasi-geostrophic omega. The two anomaly fields are departures **from the zonal mean**, because the nest's base state already carries the mean and what it cannot generate is the eddy; `frontal_strength_at` is the magnitude of the **total** thermal gradient, because removing the mean first would erase the background baroclinic zone that frontogenesis concentrates.
  - **Corrected `pressure_anomaly_hpa`, which returned the total rather than an anomaly.** It reported `rho f0 psi_2`, dominated by the mean jet's own meridional gradient — measured at **139 hPa across a 40-degree window**, an order more than any cyclone — so the editor's map would have been a picture of latitude. Now a departure from the zonal mean like its two siblings, with `QuasiGeostrophicDiagnostics::lowest_pressure_anomaly_hpa` corrected alongside it so "the deepest low in the world" names a low rather than whichever cell sits furthest poleward.
  - Reshaped authoring around what a dynamical core can honour: `IWeatherAuthoring` loses `synoptic()` and gains `inject_vorticity` — a low is a feature of a field, not an object, and what an author can do to a field is disturb it. The disturbance then deepens, tilts, sheds a front and moves with the steering flow instead of translating as a rigid shape. Presets became initial conditions rather than scripts. The read-side queries moved to `IWeatherProvider`, where every provider answers honestly, and `ISimulation` gained a const `weather_provider()` so the editor's map can sample the field without being able to disturb it.
  - **The editor's synoptic map samples a field instead of drawing a list.** It shades `pressure_anomaly_hpa` or `frontal_strength_at` on a 44×44 lattice with a 9×9 wind-arrow field over it, normalized against the extremum actually present and reporting that number, and injects an anomaly where clicked. Every slider the old panel offered — depth, radius, speed, heading — set a quantity the core now derives, and a slider that pretends to set a derived quantity is worse than no slider.
  - Scene persistence moved to a **binary sidecar** beside the scene JSON. The state is two potential-vorticity fields and a moisture field on a 512×256 grid — **1 572 920 bytes measured** — which is not going into a human-editable text file for a payload no human would edit. `q − f` is stored as float so all seven digits go to the varying part; a round trip was measured at 3×10⁻⁸ hPa, a junk blob is rejected, and a missing or wrong-grid sidecar leaves the core as seeded rather than failing the whole scene load.
  - Retired the hemisphere defect with the class that carried it: `SynopticLayer::wind_at` applied the surface-friction turn as the same rotation in both hemispheres, so every southern-hemisphere boundary wind the nest was ever given was turned the wrong way. The core turns it correctly and a unit test pins both signs.
  - Confirmed rather than fixed `Astro::Ephemeris` as the single solar authority: the nest already reads the sine of the *rendered* sun's elevation off the environment the ephemeris fills, and no second solar model survives anywhere in the simulation.
  - Named limit: the preset and click amplitudes are about 3× too strong — the editor's defaults (700 km, 20 m/s) put −70.6 hPa on the map, `FrontPassage` −94.8, `Storm` −110.9, where a deep real low is −30. `GAUSSIAN_BLOB_WIND_FACTOR` is the correct constant and the field is geostrophically self-consistent; what makes it deep is that `inject_vorticity` places a *monopole*, whose streamfunction grows logarithmically inward instead of decaying the way a compensated anomaly's does. Recorded as open — a compensated blob or simply smaller amplitudes — rather than guessed at.
  - Withdrew a wrong claim from the entry this replaced: it also said a naturally mature cyclone reads only −6.9 hPa against a textbook −31, implying the dynamics were as mis-scaled as the injection. That was measured on the default 30 m/s jet, which sits barely above the Phillips threshold. On a properly supercritical jet the core reaches **−35.4 hPa by day 8**, with the eddy energy peaking and the zonal flow drawn down — a textbook deep low. Only the injection is loud.
  - Split `TheJetsPressureAnomalyIsSynopticAndNotAstronomical`, which the corrected reference broke and which had not been re-run: a purely zonal state now asserts *exactly* zero anomaly (the claim the old total-field code failed, by 139 hPa), and the synoptic-amplitude bound moved onto a flow that has actually gone unstable.
  - Recorded a trap that cost real time: `advance()` is capped at `max_steps_per_advance` (8), so **one call can never cover more than 48 minutes of game time**. A first measurement handed it two-day intervals in a loop and concluded the core barely grew and never rained — it had simulated ten hours, not 24 days. Drive it against `simulated_seconds()`.
- 2026-07-29 — Added `sushi_atmosphere`, the engine-neutral atmosphere module, and in it **T1, the global dynamical core** (atmosphere phase C2): two-layer moist quasi-geostrophic flow on a 512×256 latitude/longitude grid, with potential vorticity and column water as the only prognostic variables and everything else — wind, pressure, vertical motion, fronts, the jet — diagnosed from them. Cyclogenesis is **emergent**: the initial state is a zonal jet plus a perturbation of a hundredth of its speed, and what grows is whatever the mean state is unstable to. Measured over 70 simulated days at full resolution: eddy kinetic energy grows exponentially at 0.26/day (0.32/day over the purely exponential days 8–12), saturates near 1.1×10⁵ J/m² against a 4.2×10⁵ J/m² zonal flow, and then runs a 15–20 day baroclinic life cycle rather than either dying or running away; peak winds reach 49 m/s and cyclones deepen to −31 hPa. One step costs 36 ms of one core and is due once per six minutes of game time.
  - Added the elliptic inversion the core is built on: the barotropic and baroclinic modes decouple **exactly**, so recovering the streamfunction is a Fourier transform in longitude and a pre-factored tridiagonal solve in latitude. The grid is cell-centred, so its polar edges carry `cos(latitude) = 0` and the boundary condition is the one the metric already supplies.
  - Added `FourierTransform`, a radix-2 transform with precomputed tables, including the pairing that rides two real rows on one complex transform — the core's two modes are exactly two, so this halves the transform cost and is not an approximation.
  - Added the quasi-geostrophic omega as a diagnosed field, from the upper layer's own vorticity budget rather than a separate equation. It replaces C1's Ekman-pumping estimate as the large-scale vertical motion the regional nest will be given, and it costs no extra solve.
  - Added `atmosphere_global_probe`, a headless driver that runs simulated weeks in seconds and fits a growth rate to the eddy energy while it is still exponential.
  - Corrected `docs/slop/atmosphere_system.md` §3.3, which planned T1 on the GPU: every consumer of the tier is host-side, so the device would have bought a step that is already nearly free and paid for it with a readback.
- 2026-07-29 — Added large-scale vertical motion to the atmosphere forcing seam (phase C1), the first term the nest receives that is not a boundary condition: it is applied across the whole domain, because a 384 km window has no way to know it is sitting under a thousand-kilometre high. Derived as **Ekman pumping** from the synoptic layer, which already carried everything needed and had nothing reading it for this — its geostrophic wind has a 25° surface-friction turn, and that turn *is* the cross-isobaric flow, so the convergence into a low and the divergence out of a high give ascent and subsidence with no tuning constant. Taken as the divergence of the near-surface wind over the Ekman layer; measured at 1.0 cm/s in the mean and ±5.5 cm/s at the centres of a 20 hPa pair, which is the textbook synoptic range. The nest applies it as vertical advection of potential temperature and vapour, so subsidence warms by compression and dries by bringing down air from where there is less water. `atmosphere_probe --parent-subsidence` measures the term in isolation.
- 2026-07-28 — Added the surface energy balance, cloud shading and ice to the Meteorology panel, the scene format and `atmosphere_probe` (phase B3e), and ran Phase B's acceptance bar. The panel now authors what a place *is* — albedo, moisture availability, heat capacity, and the mixed-phase band — and reports the fluxes, skin temperature and Bowen ratio the model solved, rather than taking two of them as input. Two of the bar's three clauses are met and emergent: a radiation fog forms overnight because the ground radiates to a cold sky, burns off at mid-morning when the sensible flux turns positive, and a fair-weather cumulus deck forms at the mixed-layer top with a base over a kilometre up. The third — evening decay — is not: the deck grows through the night, and a control run identifies the missing sink as large-scale subsidence, which a limited-area model receives from its parent rather than generating. Carried to Phase C rather than substituted for. Unit tests pin the surface balance's equilibrium and stability, the slant-path insolation, and both saturation curves against textbook values.
- 2026-07-28 — Added ice to the regional atmosphere's microphysics (phase B3d) as a diagnosed phase rather than a second condensate species. A cell's ice fraction ramps from 0 at 0 °C to 1 at −20 °C, and the saturation curve, the latent heat, the autoconversion threshold, the fall speed and the optical effective radius all follow from it — so a cold cloud condenses against the ice curve (well under the liquid one), releases 13 % more heat, precipitates at a quarter the condensate, drops something falling at a metre a second instead of seven, and renders nearly four times thinner for the same water. Measured on one forcing across three airmasses: mean coverage rises 0.430 → 0.621 as the surface cools from 288 K to 265 K *while the condensate falls*, and the cold column holds 3.5× the precipitation per unit cloud. It costs 0.5 % of the step, measured by interleaving ice-on and ice-off builds because the GPU's clocks drifted 20 % between runs. The warm case is bit-identical to before it existed, which is pinned by unit test. Named limits: no supercooled water below the glaciation point, no Bergeron *timescale*, instantaneous melting; the two-species scheme is the refinement and its cost (28 MB plus its share of the advection) is recorded.
- 2026-07-28 — Added the cloud-shading feedback to the regional atmosphere (phase B3b): a column's own optical depth now attenuates the sunlight reaching the ground under it, so a cumulus cuts the heating that lifted it. Measured by removing and restoring the term on one run — at a third-covered sky the skin runs 5.7 K cooler at solar noon, the sensible flux a third lower, and the domain peak updraft is nearly halved (0.053 → 0.029 m/s); after 14 h the column holds 17.0 kg/m² of water instead of 18.8. The column walk lives in the extinction stage, which runs once a frame rather than once a step: 0.10 ms, against one frame of staleness. Cover and optical depth are carried separately under maximum overlap, because a tenth-covered column must pass nine tenths of the sun rather than attenuate all of it weakly. Named limit: the slant path runs through the same column, so shadows fall directly under their cloud — wrong at a low sun, where the flux being shaded is small anyway.
- 2026-07-28 — Added a surface energy balance to the regional atmosphere (phase B3a): the ground now has a prognostic skin temperature and the sensible and latent fluxes are **solved** from it rather than authored. Three numbers went away — a peak sensible flux, a peak latent flux and a night-time cooling rate, scaled by the sine of the sun's elevation — and with them went the three things that made it a prescribed shape rather than a cycle: no lag (nothing had a heat capacity), no response (the ground could not react to the air it was heating), and a Bowen ratio pinned by construction. Measured over a 24 h cycle: the skin peaks **90 minutes after solar noon**, the sensible flux turns positive at solar sine +0.50 and negative at +0.065 — before sunset — and the Bowen ratio runs 0.50 → −6.25 through the day. The slab is integrated semi-implicitly so any heat capacity is stable, the exchange velocity carries Beljaars' free-convection term (without it a fixed sun drove the skin 23 K above the air), and downwelling longwave is Brutsaert's, so a humid night cools differently from a dry one. One dispatch per column: 0.069 ms, 0.7 % of the step. Editor and probe now author the *surface* — albedo, moisture availability, heat capacity — and report the fluxes it produces.
- 2026-07-28 — Added two domain-structure diagnostics to `atmosphere_probe` (`sky_coverage_sd`, `sky_coverage_roughness`) and two authored scales to the nest's surface heterogeneity (`thermal_seed_length_m`, `thermal_seed_period_s`; editor: *Patch Size*, *Patch Lifetime*). - 2026-07-28 — Added a subgrid cloud-fraction closure to the regional nest. ### Fixed
- 2026-07-29 — Fixed the synoptic layer's geostrophic wind being **735× too fast**. `V = grad(p)/(rho f)` wants pressure in pascals and `pressure_gradient` returns hectopascals per metre, so the scale is 100/1.225 = 81.6; the constant was 6.0e4, beside a comment claiming it was "tuned so a ~30 hPa deepening low reads ~15-20 m/s". Measured, the shipped `FrontPassage` preset produced a **15 534 m/s** boundary wind — the comment described the intent exactly and the number never matched it. It now produces 26 m/s. Nothing caught it because no test asserted a wind *magnitude*: the case covering this field asserted only that it was non-uniform, which a 15 km/s field satisfies perfectly. That assertion is now there, and the scale is derived from the two unit constants rather than tuned. Found while deriving Ekman pumping from the same field, where it showed as the vertical motion saturating its own cap everywhere.
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed the atmosphere nest's thermal seed doing almost nothing. It hashed the cell index and the step index, so the surface heterogeneity it stands for had no length scale but one cell, no time scale but one step, and — both being grid quantities — made the render quality tier and the frame rate into physical parameters of the weather. Measured against running with the seed switched off entirely, over simulated hours 3–6: the white field produced 1.22× the domain structure of no seed at all, and the correlated one produces 3.72×. Time is the axis that carries it (an uncorrelated kick accumulates as √N where a persistent one accumulates as N); space alone buys nothing. Now value noise on an (x, z, t) lattice addressed in world metres and game seconds, so it is anchored to the ground rather than sliding with the camera, and Medium/High/Ultra agree on domain structure to 1.10× against 2.01× before. The predicted symptom — neighbouring cells scintillating near the cloud closure's threshold — was measured and does not exist; the real one was a symmetry that never broke.
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed the atmosphere nest's query mirror freezing whenever the nest stepped continuously — which is every session that animates the sky. `collect_readback` looked for a slot whose timeline value had passed the semaphore counter, but a slot carries only its most recent submission's value and the CPU sits exactly three submissions ahead, so the value that had completed was never one a slot still held. Measured over a hundred consecutive steps: the counter read 95 while the slots held 96, 97 and 98, and nothing matched, every time. The mirror advanced only when something else idled the device, so gameplay's "two to three frames stale" (§3.2) was in fact "stale until the GPU happens to catch up". A slot is now collected immediately after the wait that precedes overwriting it, where its completion is already established: 2 readbacks per simulated hour became 1 471 of 1 478.
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed `max_steps_per_frame` capping the atmosphere nest's accumulator rather than its steps. The frame recorded exactly one step whatever the field was set to, so the nest's throughput was pinned at one step per frame and raising the number bought tolerated lag instead of weather — which is why the panel's own "animate the sky faster and less weather happens" warning had no remedy to offer. A frame now records every step that is due, bounded by the cap and again by what the recording slot's descriptor pool can serve; the thermal seed's step index moved to a push constant so steps sharing one parameter upload do not draw the identical pattern.
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed the atmosphere nest's ~12.6 ms step landing inside the frame that reads it — a dropped frame every 2.4 s at 1× time scale, and four times that once a frame could record four steps. Each scene view waited on the step submitted during its own frame, so the queue it ran on was irrelevant. The step is now staged during the frame and submitted at its end, after every view; the frame reads the previous step and waits on a value that has almost always already passed, and the step waits on the frame's readers in turn (submission order on a queue orders the start of submissions, not their completion). Costs one nest step of staleness, a couple of seconds of game time.
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed the atmosphere nest's time step being pinned three times shorter than anything the model needed. The vertical CFL divided by a flat 20 m/s — a thunderstorm core — in every airmass and on every grid, while the measured domain peak is 0.02 m/s in fair weather and 0.03 in a convecting one; and since the transport is semi-Lagrangian and unconditionally stable, what that bought was not stability but a vertical Courant number of 9e-4, which is the maximally diffusive regime the scheme exists to avoid. It also meant `convective_velocity_scale`, documented as "purely a reporting scale", was quietly setting the time step. The CFL now binds against the updraft the readback measures, with fourfold headroom and a decay so it tightens at once and relaxes slowly. Measured unchanged through 6 s in both regimes (sky coverage within 3 %, cloud base within 2 %) and visibly wrong at 10 s. The step goes 2.43 → 6.0 s, so the tier's cost per simulated second goes 5.75 → 1.27 ms — 0.021 ms a frame at 1× time scale, and 4.5× less than at the start of the day for the same weather.
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed the atmosphere nest spending five of its twelve pressure sweeps on nothing. Twelve was chosen on the reasoning that a solver without a coarse-grid correction leaves smooth horizontal error behind — true of an isotropic Poisson problem, and beside the point on a grid that is 2 km horizontally against 54–560 m vertically, where the line solver inverts the dominant direction exactly and what is left is a small, strongly diagonally dominant correction. Measured end-to-end over six simulated hours: at 2, 4, 8, 12 and 20 sweeps the surface humidity, condensation level, cloud base, column water and sky coverage are identical to every printed figure and the peak divergence agrees to seven significant figures; at one sweep they are not, which is what tells convergence from a solve that never mattered. Holds under a 25 m/s front. `pressure_iterations` now defaults to 4, taking the pressure stage from 7.7 to 2.7 ms and the whole step from 12.6 to 7.95 ms.
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed the atmosphere nest's pressure relaxation recomputing per column what depends only on height. The tridiagonal matrix is a function of the level index alone — base-state density, potential temperature and the stretched grid's spacings do not vary horizontally — so its Thomas factorization is now built once per workgroup into shared memory instead of once per column into a dynamically indexed 64-float private array that spilled to local memory, and the base-state `pow`/`exp` chain is evaluated 64× less. 8.90 → 7.71 ms with the physics bit-identical.
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed the atmosphere nest's vertical diffusivity weakening exactly where it had to be strongest. The parabolic profile went as `K_peak·(z/h)` near the ground, so a *deeper* mixed layer mixed its own surface more weakly — and the lowest face carries the entire surface flux out of a 54 m level. Measured: 12 m²/s there, the surface level 9 K above the one 80 m up, the layer never homogenising, and its top reaching 57 % relative humidity after eight hours of heating. Replaced with Troen & Mahrt (1986), `K = κ·w_s·z·(1 − z/h)²`, whose near-surface slope does not depend on the layer depth; `boundary_layer_diffusivity` (m²/s) accordingly becomes `boundary_layer_velocity_scale` (m/s).
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed the atmosphere nest's thermal seed being an unbounded random walk. It added a fresh perturbation to potential temperature every step, so across 37 000 columns its cold tail kept a few percent of the domain in permanent ground fog which the sky then reported as cloud. It now modulates the surface heat flux, which is where surface heterogeneity physically lives: bounded by construction, and it stops at dusk with the flux it scales. `thermal_seed_amplitude` becomes a dimensionless patchiness (editor: *Surface Patchiness*) rather than a rate in K/s.
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed a level that is momentarily colder than the one above it decoupling from the atmosphere nest's boundary layer entirely: the parcel test correctly found the column stable, diagnosed zero mixing depth, and left the level exchanging with nothing — so the thermal seed's random walk accumulated in it without limit, measured at −8.76 K after four hours at 99 % relative humidity while the level above sat at 50 %. The diagnosed depth now carries a floor of the two lowest levels, which is the surface layer's mechanical turbulence and does not switch off with the stratification. Every cloud the nest made before this was that fog.
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed the cloudscape bake eroding the nest path's *density* instead of its *shape*, which turned any thin deck into flickering specks. The fringe erosion subtracts an absolute threshold averaging 0.225, written for the deck path where density is an authored quantity of order one; the nest path's density is measured, and a marginal fair-weather deck (14 % cloud fraction, 0.01 g/kg, sitting at the closure's critical humidity) arrives at 0.058 — so the erosion drove 87 % of its texels to zero and left the noise's own peaks, which changed every rebake. The carve and the erosion now run on the dimensionless shape and the measured water multiplies once at the end.
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed the Meteorology panel's clock verdict and its "Match sky to atmosphere" button assuming 60 fps. A session running at about twelve was told "the atmosphere is keeping up with the sky" while four of every five seconds of weather were being discarded. Both now use the measured ratio of weather asked for to weather simulated, over a recent window rather than the session total so that correcting the rate actually converges.
- 2026-07-28 — Fixed the atmosphere nest's cloud base and top being found by an optical-depth threshold while coverage came from the cloud fraction.
